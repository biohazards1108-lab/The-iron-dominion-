⚔ Core Rules

  1. No cheating or exploits. Do not use unauthorized clients, x-ray tools, dupes, or exploits.
  2. No griefing. Do not intentionally destroy or sabotage another player's builds or property.
  3. Respect other players. Harassment, threats, hate speech, and targeted abuse are not acceptable.
  4. Respect staff. Follow reasonable staff instructions and use the appropriate support channel for disputes.
  5. Keep chat usable. No spam, malicious advertising, or disruptive behavior.
  6. Do not abuse server systems. Attempts to manipulate economy, ranks, crates, claims, or other systems through unintended behavior may result in action.

🛡 Fair Play

Iron Dominion is designed around survival, technology, progression, and player competition. The goal is to create a world where players can build powerful operations without relying on cheating or exploits.

⚖ Enforcement

Penalties depend on severity and history and may include warnings, temporary restrictions, rollbacks, or bans. Staff may act immediately when necessary to protect the server or other players.

Rules evolve. As new systems are added, this page will be updated to match the actual server rules.
