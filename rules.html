⚔ Core community standards

  1. Respect others. Harassment, threats, hate speech, and targeted abuse are not acceptable.
  2. No cheating or exploitation. Do not use unauthorized clients, exploits, duplication glitches, or attempts to bypass server protections.
  3. Respect builds and property. Do not grief, steal, or sabotage other players unless a clearly published game mode explicitly permits that behavior.
  4. Keep chat constructive. Avoid spam, malicious links, scams, and disruptive advertising.
  5. Protect accounts. Never share passwords, authentication codes, or private account information.
  6. Respect staff and appeals. Follow reasonable moderation instructions and use official channels to raise concerns.
  7. Do not abuse systems. Attempts to manipulate future ranks, shops, rewards, or economy systems may result in moderation.

🛡 Enforcement and updates

Moderation may include warnings, temporary restrictions, rollbacks, or bans depending on severity and context. Final in-game rules will be reviewed and updated before launch to match the actual server configuration.

These are community guidelines for the project; any mode-specific rules will be published before that mode opens.
