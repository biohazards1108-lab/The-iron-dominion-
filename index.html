🏰 What Kind of Server Is Iron Dominion?

Iron Dominion is built for players who want more than a basic survival world. The core experience combines classic survival with Tekkit's industrial technology, a player-driven economy, rank progression, exploration, and a world designed to encourage players to build their own settlements and empires.

⛏ Survival

Leave the protected spawn hub, find your territory, gather resources, and build a base that can grow into something much larger.

⚙ Technology

Automate production, generate power, process resources, and push your technology further as your operation grows.

🏰 Player Empires

Create your own city, industrial complex, trading operation, or settlement and leave your mark on the Dominion.

🧭 Your Journey

1. Enter the Dominion
Start at the main spawn hub, learn the server, and access markets and services.
2. Enter Survival
Use the Survival Gate to leave the hub and begin your wilderness journey.
3. Build Your Future
Mine, automate, trade, explore, and establish your own place in the world.
4. Help Shape the Server
Vote, report problems, suggest features, and help the Dominion evolve.

🔨 Constantly Evolving

Iron Dominion is actively developed. New systems, builds, balancing changes, events, quality-of-life improvements, and community ideas can continue shaping the experience after launch.

🛠 Development

We'd rather improve the server continuously than call it finished and stop listening.
