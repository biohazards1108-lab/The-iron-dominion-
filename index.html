🏰 Welcome to Iron Dominion

Iron Dominion is a technology-driven Tekkit survival experience built around exploration, industry, trade, progression, and player-created settlements.

⛏ Survive

Enter the wilderness, establish a base, gather resources, and build your own future.

⚙ Engineer

Use Tekkit technology to automate production, generate power, and build increasingly advanced systems.

💰 Trade

Mine valuable resources, participate in the economy, and rise through the Dominion.

🧭 Your Journey

1. Enter the Dominion
Start at the main spawn hub, learn the basics, and explore the server's services.
2. Enter Survival
Use the Survival Gate to leave the hub and begin your survival journey.
3. Claim Your Future
Build, mine, automate, trade, and develop your own place in the world.
4. Rise Through the Ranks
Progress through the Dominion economy and server systems as they expand.
