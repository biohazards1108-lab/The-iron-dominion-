110 Tekkit mods + IndustrialCraft 2

Iron Dominion uses the 110 mods included with the Tekkit pack, with IndustrialCraft 2 (IC2) added separately by Iron Dominion. That means players need the normal Tekkit pack plus the correct IC2 build for Minecraft 1.6.4.

Base pack: Tekkit 1.2.9i • Minecraft: 1.6.4 • Additional mod: IndustrialCraft 2
