Scope and service providers

This notice covers the Iron Dominion public website and its account sign-up and sign-in features when enabled. GitHub Pages or other hosting providers may process technical request data such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, and requested paths. GitHub and Discord have separate privacy policies for their services.

Iron Dominion accounts

If you create or use an Iron Dominion account, the authentication service provider Supabase processes your email address, authentication credentials, account identifiers, and security/session information to register you, verify your email, sign you in, and support password recovery. Passwords are submitted to Supabase's authentication service; the website code does not intentionally store plaintext passwords in its own website files or database. Review Supabase's privacy policy for its handling of that information.

The website may keep authentication session information in your browser so you can remain signed in. Use a private device you trust and sign out on shared devices. Never give Iron Dominion your Microsoft password. Microsoft OAuth is provided by Supabase and Microsoft; Iron Dominion does not receive your Microsoft password. Signing in with Microsoft does not by itself verify Minecraft ownership.

Cookies, storage, and analytics

The account flow uses browser session storage mechanisms provided by the authentication client to maintain sign-in state. The site does not claim to operate a custom advertising profile or analytics program. Hosting and third-party services may have their own logs, cookies, or technologies.

Minecraft and server information

Profile usernames, display names, and dashboard layout preference may be stored in the Supabase profile table after the account-profile migration is applied. Minecraft account linking, vote history, server rank, Dominion Token balances, and live player statistics remain unavailable until connected to trusted production data sources. The dashboard must show unavailable values rather than invent them. Do not treat an unavailable field as a record of your activity.

Payments and purchases

The website store is not open and this site does not currently take payments. Do not send payment details through Discord or any unofficial channel.

Your choices and questions

Do not create an account if you do not agree to the processing described above. For account assistance or privacy questions, contact the project through the official Discord. Account deletion and data-request procedures should be confirmed before public launch. This is a plain-language project notice, not legal advice.
