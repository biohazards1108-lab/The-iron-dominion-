What donations support

TRANSPARENCY

Donations are support-only for now. No items, ranks, currency, or other in-game rewards are granted for donating at this time. Future updates WILL introduce rewards for voting, donating, and purchasing rank packages. Those systems will be announced with their rules and benefits before they launch; future rewards are not available today.

Protect your money: do not pay individuals in direct messages claiming to represent Iron Dominion. We will publish payment links only on theirondominion.com after verifying ownership of the receiving accounts.

How secure donations will work

  • Payments will be handled by the provider's hosted checkout or official payment flow—not by a form that collects card details on this website.
  • Private API keys and payment secrets will stay in server-side environment variables, never in public HTML or GitHub.
  • If donations are ever tied to a rank, item, or other in-game benefit, fulfillment will require verified server-side payment confirmation and duplicate-payment protection.
  • Cash App peer-to-peer transfers will not automatically unlock game rewards. Any reward-bearing checkout must use a supported merchant integration with server-side confirmation.
  • We will test the payment flow and refund/support process before enabling live payments.

Future community rewards

Future updates WILL add reward systems for voting, donating, and buying rank packages. These features are still in development; no reward is granted for any of these activities right now. We will publish the exact benefits and rules before any system goes live.
