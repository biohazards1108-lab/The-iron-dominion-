Official support channel

For project questions and community assistance, visit the Iron Dominion Discord. Support availability and response times are not guaranteed.

What to include

  • A short description of the issue.
  • Relevant error text or a screenshot with private details hidden.
  • Your game edition and version, if relevant.

Protect yourself

  • Never share passwords, authentication codes, recovery codes, or payment card details.
  • We do not currently accept store purchases through this website.
  • Beware of impersonators, unofficial payment requests, and fake giveaways.

Report a security concern

For website security issues, follow the instructions in our security contact policy. Please avoid publicly posting exploit details before a fix is available.
