🎁 Buy Crates with Dominion Tokens

📊 How it Works

  1. View available crates and their prices in Dominion Tokens
  2. Click "Buy Crate" and enter your in-game username
  3. The purchase is processed and tokens are deducted from your account
  4. Your items are delivered to your in-game inventory or mail

How to Sell

Open a shop stall in the market area and use the chest/shop plugin to manage sales. (Details depend on server plugins.)
