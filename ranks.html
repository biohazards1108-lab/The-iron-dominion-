📜 TERMS OF THE FORGE

Rank Questions & Rules

When does the 30-day timer start?

Only after the server receives and verifies the payment and the rank is successfully activated. A checkout return page alone must never grant a rank.

How does the 25% renewal discount work?

The proposed rule is a 25% discount if the same rank is renewed during the 15 calendar days after its 30-day term expires. After that window ends, its full then-current price applies. The checkout should show the exact expiry and discount deadline.

Do higher ranks include previous ranks?

Yes, this draft uses cumulative tiers: each higher rank includes the perks listed for every lower tier, plus its own additions. The technical implementation must avoid duplicate kits and duplicate permissions.

Will ranks change in-game chat?

Proposed chat features include rank-colored prefixes and name styling. This depends on a compatible Bedrock server plugin/add-on or server-side chat system. It must be tested before being advertised as a working benefit.

Can I buy a rank right now?

No. The store and rank fulfillment system are not open. No purchase links or payment controls are enabled on this page.
