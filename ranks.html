Rank Progression

  • Citizen — New players, basic permissions.
  • Knight — Access to starter machines and shops.
  • Noble — Better factory permissions and land claims.
  • Elite — Access to advanced tech and reserved auction slots.
  • Master — Can create guild factories and tax members.
  • Legend — Top-tier benefits, server recognition.
  • Dominion — Reserved for founders and elite staff.

How to Rank Up

Rank progress is based on a combination of playtime, contributions, and economy standings. Participate in events and economy to rise faster.
